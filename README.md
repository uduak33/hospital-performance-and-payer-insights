# Hospital Performance and Payer Insights
## Operational, Clinical, and Financial Insights from Synthetic EHR Data

A healthcare analytics portfolio project simulating real-world hospital decision-making using SQL and Tableau.
This analysis focuses on clinical efficiency, financial exposure, and service utilisation patterns to support leadership-level planning and strategy.

## Business Context

Hospitals operate in an environment of increasing clinical complexity, financial pressure, and fluctuating patient demand across various care settings. Leadership teams rely on timely, well-designed analytics to understand where inefficiencies exist, which patient populations drive cost and risk, and how service demand is evolving.

Healthcare analysts play a critical role in translating electronic health record (EHR) data into actionable insights that inform clinical operations, payer strategy, and capacity planning.

This project simulates how an analyst embedded within a hospital or health insurance organisation would assess performance using EHR-derived data, to support evidence-based decision-making rather than retrospective reporting.

## Objectives

The objectives of this analysis are to:
1. Provide a high-level executive overview of hospital activity, outcomes, and financial exposure
2. Identify diagnosis-level drivers of prolonged length of stay and hospital readmissions
3. Assess payer mix and uninsured exposure contributing to uncompensated care
4. Examine diagnosis and procedure utilisation patterns across encounter settings
5. Analyse longitudinal encounter trends to inform capacity planning and service demand forecasting

Dashboards and insights are structured to reflect how hospital leadership and payer stakeholders consume analytics in practice.
